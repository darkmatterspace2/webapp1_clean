💡 Search Operator Reference

site:example.com Restrict results exclusively to a domain or subdomain.
-site:domain.com Exclude specific domain or spam platform from results.
filetype:pdf / ext:pdf Limit results to specific document or file extension.
intitle:"exact term" Require word or exact phrase in the webpage title.
inurl:keyword Require keyword anywhere within URL address slug.
intext:"exact phrase" Require phrase in webpage body text.
AROUND(n) Find pages where two terms are within n words of each other.
"exact phrase" Match precise wording in order without synonym expansion.
